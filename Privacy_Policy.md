# Privacy Policy for Pick-A-Pill

**Last updated: June 28, 2026**

## Overview

Pick-A-Pill is a medication tracking application designed to help you keep track of your courses of medication, dosing schedules, and adherence history. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and — most importantly — what it does *not* do with that information.

**The short version: Pick-A-Pill does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data with anyone, because the app has no internet connection, no servers, and no third parties involved at all.**

---

## 1. What Information the App Stores

Pick-A-Pill stores the following information, entered directly by you, entirely on your own device:

- **Profile information:** a display name you choose during setup (e.g. "Sarah"), used only to personalize greetings within the app.
- **Medication and course data:** medication names, dosage strengths, forms (pill, liquid, injection), frequency and scheduling preferences, and reminder times that you enter.
- **Dose history:** records of which scheduled doses you have marked as taken, missed, or pending, used to calculate your adherence statistics and streaks.
- **App preferences:** settings such as whether notifications are enabled and your preferred refill reminder window.

No other personal information is requested or stored. The app does not ask for your email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any government-issued identifiers.

---

## 2. Where Your Data Lives

All data listed above is stored **exclusively on your device**, using an encrypted local database. Specifically:

- Data is stored locally using encrypted on-device storage, with the encryption key generated and held securely on your device.
- There is no cloud backup, no server-side database, and no remote storage of any kind associated with this app.
- The app does not require, and cannot use, an internet connection to function. It has no networking code at all.

**In practical terms: your medication data never leaves your phone.** It is not sent anywhere, ever, under any circumstance, because there is no mechanism in the app capable of sending it anywhere.

---

## 3. Who Can Access Your Data

Because your data stays only on your device:

- **We (the developer) cannot see your data.** There is no account system, no login, and no way for us to access, view, or retrieve any information you enter into the app.
- **No third parties have access.** The app does not integrate with any analytics providers, advertising networks, crash-reporting services, or other third-party SDKs that could collect or transmit your data.
- **Anyone with physical access to your unlocked device can view the app's contents.** Pick-A-Pill does not currently include an app-level PIN or biometric lock separate from your device's own lock screen. If you share your device with others, be mindful that anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app and view your medication information. (An optional in-app lock is under consideration for a future update.)

---

## 4. Notifications

Pick-A-Pill uses your device's local notification system to remind you when a dose is due. These reminders are scheduled and triggered entirely on your device — no notification content is ever sent through any external server (which is how notifications work on many apps that rely on push notification services; Pick-A-Pill does not, since reminders are scheduled locally on your device in advance).

To protect your privacy in shared or public settings, medication names and dosage details are **not displayed on your lock screen** — notifications shown while your device is locked display only a generic reminder message. Full details are visible only after you unlock your device.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

- Your data remains stored on your device for as long as the app is installed.
- **Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all associated data.** There is no backup copy anywhere else, so uninstalling cannot be undone — please be sure before you do so if your data matters to you.
- Clearing the app's storage through your device's Android settings (Settings → Apps → Pick-A-Pill → Storage → Clear Data) will also permanently erase all stored information.
- Because there is no server-side copy, we have no ability to recover deleted data on your behalf under any circumstances.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Pick-A-Pill does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, since it does not collect information from or about anyone in the first place beyond what a user voluntarily types into their own local copy of the app. The app is not directed at children and is intended for general use in managing personal medication schedules.

---
